<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/amend-the-sentence3235/1">Convert CamelCase to Sentence</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given a string <strong>s</strong> containing multiple words concatenated together, where each new word starts with an uppercase letter, insert spaces between the words and convert all characters to lowercase.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>s = "geeksForGeeks"
<strong>Output:</strong> "geeks for geeks"<br><strong>Explanation</strong>: The words in the string are "geeks", "For", "Geeks". After inserting spaces before each word and converting all characters to lowercase, the resulting sentence is "geeks for geeks".</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input</strong>: s = "You"
<strong>Output:</strong> "you"
<strong>Explanation</strong>: The only word in the string is "You". After inserting spaces before each word and converting all characters to lowercase, the resulting sentence is "you".
</span></pre>
